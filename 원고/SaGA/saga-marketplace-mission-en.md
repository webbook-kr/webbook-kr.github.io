# Why Marketplace Mission | Sunday's faith, Monday's decisions

## Why Marketplace Mission, Why Now

Where we spend most of our week ==is the front line of God's Kingdom==.

- **Work Is a Calling** More than a living — a place of divine calling
- **Work Is Worship** Worshipping God on Monday, not only on Sunday
- **Business Is a Kingdom Platform** Business as Mission — a channel of godly influence

> Faith must not stay inside Sunday — it belongs in Monday's meetings, markets and decisions.

## A Training Ground for Workplace Leaders

The SaGA School of Marketplace Mission & Global Network is a core programme of SaRang Global Academy at SaRang Community Church, Seoul. It equips whole-life disciples of Jesus Christ to serve in the workplace and the marketplace.

- **Workplace Missionary** Leaders who serve their workplace and profession as a mission field
- **Missional Entrepreneur** Business leaders who build their enterprises as platforms for the Kingdom of God
- **Global Mission Networker** Co-labouring in marketplace mission across nations and industries

## Our Aim

핵심: To raise workplace missionaries and missional entrepreneurs who bring spiritual vitality to the workplace and extend the Kingdom of God through godly influence.

# BGU | Bakke Graduate University

## Learning into Degrees, Ministry into Expertise

Transformational leadership at an accredited U.S. graduate school.

핵심: 12 credits recognised — credit transfer

## A Graduate School for Transformational Leadership

Bakke Graduate University (BGU) is a U.S. graduate school that develops leaders for the transformation of cities, workplaces and communities. In partnership with Korean leaders, it offers degree programmes taught in Korean and contextualised for Korea.

- **Accredited U.S. Degrees** Accredited by TRACS and the Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board
- **Taught in Korean** A curriculum contextualised for Korea
- **Practice-Centered Learning** Research and solve real problems in cities and workplaces

## MATL and DTL

- **MATL · Master's Degree** Master of Arts in Transformational Leadership. A master's programme in the theory and practice of leadership that transforms workplaces and communities.
- **DTL · Doctoral Degree** Doctor of Transformational Leadership. BGU's flagship degree: a professional doctorate that applies research to real-world problems.
